# Lingua

> **Speak it. Search it. Understand it. — Without leaving the app.**

**Lingua** is a fast, offline-first language companion built to help you understand words the moment you encounter them.

Instead of switching between apps, opening a browser, or searching multiple platforms, Lingua puts everything in one place — **speak a word, type it, search for it, and instantly understand what it means.**

## ✨ What Lingua Does

* 🎙️ **Speak a word** — Say any word and get its meaning instantly.
* 🔎 **Instant search** — Search for words without leaving the app.
* 📖 **Definitions & meanings** — Quickly understand unfamiliar words.
* 🌐 **Language support** — Explore words across different languages.
* 📴 **Works offline** — Core features remain available without an internet connection.
* ⚡ **Fast & lightweight** — Designed to get you the answer with minimal friction.
* 🧠 **More than a dictionary** — Built as a complete language-learning and exploration tool.
* 🔊 **Pronunciation** — Hear how words are spoken.
* 📝 **Word information** — Explore useful details about the word instead of just getting a one-line definition.
* 📚 **Build your vocabulary** — Discover and learn new words as you go.

## 🎯 The Idea

We've all had that moment:

> *"What does this word mean?"*

Then you open a browser → search Google → open a dictionary → find the result → go back to what you were doing.

**Lingua removes all of that friction.**

Just:

```text
🎙️ "Ephemeral"
        ↓
     Lingua
        ↓
"Existing for a very short time."
```

Or simply type:

```text
Search → Ephemeral
```

and get the information immediately.

## 📴 Offline First

Lingua isn't designed around the assumption that you'll always have an internet connection.

Its offline capabilities allow you to keep exploring words even when you're:

* ✈️ Traveling
* 🚇 On the subway
* 🏕️ Away from Wi-Fi
* 🌐 Without mobile data
* 💻 Working completely offline

Your dictionary shouldn't stop working just because your internet does.
